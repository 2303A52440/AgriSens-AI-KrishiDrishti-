Phase 1 Prototype System Architecture

📷

1. Image Acquisition & Edge Tensor Prep

Captures foliage via camera or file upload, normalizes RGB tensors to 224x224, and executes ONNX INT8 quantized model inference locally in under 15ms.

🧮

2. Severity Surface Segmentation

Performs HSV leaf lesion segmentation to calculate affected leaf surface area (0-100%), aiding precise dosage estimation for smallholder acreage.

🗣️

3. Multilingual Local Voice Advisory

Converts organic, chemical, and cultural remedies into native speech across 9 Indian languages (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, etc.) using offline TTS.
